PINDROP - EASY OPEN EDITION

1. Extract this entire ZIP.
2. Double-click index.html. Open with Chrome or Edge if prompted.

No Node.js, package installation, or terminal commands are needed.
This is the current React interface, bundled into one HTML file.

Includes the map, categories, sample pins, pin creation, search, saved pins,
Bay Area neighborhoods and navigation controls. Map tiles and route previews
need internet. GPS and compass depend on your browser and device permissions.

Pins are saved in this browser, not shared with other people.
Accounts and community sync require the full server version.
The Bay Area sample listings are fictional.

Build and syntax checks passed. Direct file opening could not be checked by
the automated browser tool; please try index.html in your normal browser.
